# Design Context

Last updated: 2026-09-01 (eval fixture)

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Ember Coffee Roasters
- **Tagline:** Roasted slow. Poured bright.
- **Mission:** Make specialty coffee feel warm and unpretentious
- **Industry:** Specialty coffee / DTC subscription

## Typography
- **Heading Font:** "Fraunces", Georgia, serif
- **Body Font:** "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif
- **Mono Font:** "JetBrains Mono", monospace
- **Base Size:** 16px
- **Scale Ratio:** 1.25
- **Heading Weight:** 600
- **Body Weight:** 400

## Design Style
- **Primary Style:** Organic
- **Border Radius:** 12px
- **Shadow Style:** subtle
- **Spacing System:** 8px base grid

## Audience
- **Primary:** Coffee enthusiasts 25-45 who buy beans online
- **Aesthetic:** Warm, crafted, editorial
- **Tone:** Friendly and knowledgeable, never snobby

## Brand Rules
- **Always:** Sentence case headlines
- **Never:** Stock photos of latte art, pure black backgrounds
